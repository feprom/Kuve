The service

Your account, your capital

A bank term deposit pays an agreed rate. Self-Custodied DPF is the trade name of an automated futures strategy with variable returns and a risk of loss.

Binance account
You control deposits and withdrawals. The exchange holds your balance and positions.
KUVE bot
The API key allows account queries and futures trading with withdrawals disabled.

Markets

The allocation aims to limit concentration and drawdowns. Multiple asset classes can fall at the same time.

Trading logic

From candle close to order

The bot follows four steps at each hourly candle close.

  1. Reads the market

    Looks for price breakouts and filters the trend with an exponential moving average.

  2. Sizes the position

    Sizes the position using observed volatility and profile limits.

  3. Trades or waits

    Based on the signal, it may go long, go short, or stay out.

  4. Manages the exit

    Exits using price-channel rules or a volatility-based trailing stop.

Sideways market

The bot may stay out when a breakout is unconfirmed. False breakouts can lead to losses.

Trending market

Looks for sustained moves in either direction while the signal holds.
